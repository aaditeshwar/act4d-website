# SIV 861, Fall 2010: Introduction to ICTD - Short

**Short module, Fall 2010**

This course will introduce students to the use of information and communication technologies for development. Most teaching will be through case studies of projects in different areas such as communication networks, education, healthcare, and community media.

**Prerequisites:**None.

**Contact hours:**14

**Grading:**

- Paper presentations, rebuttals, and reviews: Each student will present one or two papers to the rest of the class, and another student will rebutt the paper. All students will also review the paper as done for conference submissions.
